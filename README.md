# Freetown Flash Flood Alert Network (F3-Alert)

### 1. Overview
F3-Alert is a proposed early warning system designed to monitor rainfall and rising water levels in Freetown. It uses sensors to detect flood risks and sends alerts to vulnerable communities.

### 2. Rationale
The project aims to reduce flood-related deaths and property damage by providing early warnings and supporting timely emergency responses.

### 3. Problem Description
Freetown experiences flooding due to heavy rainfall, blocked drainage systems, poor waste management and rapid urbanisation. These conditions put lives and properties at risk.

### 4. Background
Flooding is a recurring environmental challenge in Freetown, particularly in low-lying communities such as Kroo Bay, Susan’s Bay and Lumley. There is a need for improved flood monitoring and early warning systems.

### 5. Community Impact and Beneficiaries
The system will benefit residents of flood-prone communities, local authorities, emergency responders and business owners by improving preparedness and reducing potential losses.

### 6. Proposed Solutions
F3-Alert will use ultrasonic water-level sensors and rainfall sensors to monitor environmental conditions. It will send SMS alerts and activate warning sirens when dangerous conditions are detected.

### 7. Computational Thinking Process
The project applies four computational thinking concepts:
- **Decomposition:** Dividing the system into smaller modules.
- **Pattern Recognition:** Identifying changes in rainfall and water levels.
- **Abstraction:** Focusing on important flood-risk information.
- **Algorithm Design:** Developing clear steps for detecting and reporting flood risks.

### 8. Algorithm Design
The system collects sensor readings, checks the data, evaluates flood risks, classifies the situation and sends appropriate warnings. Monitoring repeats every 60 seconds.

### 14. Main System Module
This module starts the system, collects sensor readings, checks flood conditions and repeats monitoring every 60 seconds.

### 15. Evaluate Risk Function
This function examines water-level and rainfall readings and determines the appropriate flood-risk level.

### 16. Risk Classification
The system classifies flood risks into three levels:

| Level | Condition |
|---|---|
| Normal | Water level below 1.8m and rainfall below 45mm/hr |
| Alert | Water level reaches 1.8m or rainfall reaches 45mm/hr |
| Critical Evacuation | Water level reaches or exceeds 2.5m |

*These are proposed project thresholds and require local validation before deployment.*

### 17. Report Procedures
The system records sensor readings, dates, times and risk levels. It generates reports to help authorities monitor flood conditions and plan emergency responses.
